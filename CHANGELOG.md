# Changelog

## Unreleased

**New**
- **Get Out, Sugar**: Trixie, the host from Chairface's Casino, yells at you before you die. 17 warnings, about twenty lines each:
  - **Danger:** standing in something, a big hit, you pulled aggro, about to pull aggro, tank losing a monster, stunned or feared, a boss picking you, boss warnings, drowning, fatigue.
  - **Moments:** you died, boss fight starts, lost or won, gear about to break, ready checks, out of range or not facing.
- Every warning starts off. `/trixie` opens the options: tick what you want and press Play to hear it. Mute, the sound channel, and how often she speaks are there too.
- A danger line cuts off a less urgent one, and each warning waits out a cooldown before repeating.
- `/trixie probe` records what this client lets the warnings see, for fine-tuning.
- For WoW Forever. The client has no combat log, so "standing in something" is a best guess from the damage you take, and can mistake a damage-over-time spell for fire.
- The voice lines are placeholders spoken by a Windows voice while the addon is tested; Trixie's real voice comes next.
